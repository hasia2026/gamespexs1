## What your venue gets

We bring a complete game-research session to your location. Up to **10 players (18+)** play,
rate, and answer a short post-game survey on our tablets. A few days later you receive a
**one-page findings summary**: what they played, what they loved, how engaged they were —
with session photos.

**We bring:** the games, the tablets, the crew, the consent paperwork.
**You bring:** a room with tables and power, and 60–180 minutes.
**You do:** nothing else. Your staff never touches the research.

## What's included in the $333

- 10 verified player seats — a full session block at your location
- The full GAMESPEXS quadrant lineup: team ball · video · board · card & odd
- Post-game surveys on our tablets (questions can't be skipped, so the data is real)
- Session photos for your own marketing
- One-page findings summary, delivered within a week

## Where the money goes — we can show you

Every package dollar is split automatically, and a permanent receipt exists for each part:

| Share | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| ⅓ | The player side — your community members who play |
| ⅓ | The interview crew running your session |
| ⅓ | The GAMESPEXS platform |

No invoice math, no hourly billing. One price, and you can audit where it went.

## Why be a pilot partner

- **Charter status.** Pilot partners get first access to sponsor packages and a say in the
  research agenda we run at your venue.
- **Sponsor-ready.** Sponsors pay for measured engagement. Your pilot makes your venue the
  first with real engagement numbers.
- **Zero risk.** One session. If it isn't worth it, you never book again — and the $100
  charity donation still happened.
